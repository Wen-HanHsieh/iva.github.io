IVA: Instruct-Verify-and-Act

Teaching Vision-Language-Action Models to Reject the Impossible

Wen-Han Hsieh*, Elvis Hsieh*, Dantong Niu, Trevor Darrell, Roei Herzig, David M. Chan
University of California, Berkeley * equal contribution
arXiv PDF Code & Data

Abstract

IVA is a unified framework for Vision-Language-Action models that detects when an instruction is unfulfillable (false premise), clarifies or corrects it in natural language, and then acts safely. Trained with paired true- and false-premise instructions, IVA improves false-premise handling while maintaining strong true-premise task performance. See the paper for details.
